# AI Use Disclosure

This project was developed with the assistance of generative AI tools.

## How AI was used

AI assistants were used as development aids for tasks such as:

- writing and refactoring code;
- debugging and suggesting fixes;
- drafting documentation, comments and README content;
- generating test cases and examples.

## Human oversight

All AI-generated or AI-assisted content was reviewed, modified where necessary, and tested by the author before inclusion. Design decisions, the choice of methods and the validation of results remain the responsibility of the author.

## What AI was not used for

- No case data, evidence, personal data or confidential information was submitted to AI services during development.
- The tool does not send data to AI services at runtime, unless explicitly stated elsewhere in this repository.
- AI was not used to make investigative or evidentiary judgements.

## Note for forensic use

As with any forensic software, this tool should be validated by the user in their own environment before being used on real cases. Results should be verified against independent methods or tools where possible, and the analyst remains responsible for the interpretation of any output.

For questions about this disclosure or about the development process, please open an issue in this repository.
